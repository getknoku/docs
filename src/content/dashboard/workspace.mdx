# Workspace & billing

Workspace settings apply across all projects in your organization.

Open the workspace menu from the dashboard sidebar.

## Trial and plan

New workspaces start with a 14-day Business trial. No credit card is required.

During the trial, the workspace has Business features such as private knowledge connectors, the support form deflector, Business analytics, and weekly digest.

If the trial ends without a payment method, the workspace moves to Free. Add a card under Billing before the trial ends to continue on Business.

## Billing

Billing shows:

- Current plan
- Message and page usage
- Upgrade, downgrade, and annual billing options
- Overage settings on Business and above
- Invoices

Pricing details live at [knoku.com/pricing](https://knoku.com/pricing).

## Members and roles

Use **Settings > Members** to invite teammates, remove access, and assign roles.

Use **Settings > Roles** to create granular permissions for sources, documents, analytics, settings, API keys, and other project areas.

Seat limits depend on the workspace plan.

## SSO

SSO is available on Enterprise.

Use **Settings > SSO** to verify your email domain, connect a SAML identity provider, and optionally require SSO for users on that domain.

## Audit log

Audit log is available on Enterprise.

Use **Settings > Audit log** to review timestamped organization changes, including member changes, billing changes, SSO changes, and integration updates.

## Weekly digest

Weekly digest is available on Business and above.

Use **Settings** (Weekly digest card) to send workspace admins a Monday email with usage, top questions, coverage gaps, and plan usage.
