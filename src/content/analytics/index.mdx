# Analytics

Knoku analytics turns sessions into a doc roadmap: where users come from, which answers worked, what questions keep repeating, and which sources were cited.

Open **Project > Analytics**.

## Analytics views

| View | Use it for | More |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summary | Message volume, sessions, deflection, helpful rate, and channel mix | This page |
| Traffic | Country and city distribution when geographic collection is enabled | [Traffic](/analytics/traffic) |
| Deflection | Answered vs needs-support outcomes and support form saves | [Deflection](/analytics/deflection) |
| Questions | Coverage gaps and frequent question clusters | [Questions](/analytics/questions) |
| Sources | Documents that were cited, and documents that were not used | [Sources cited](/analytics/sources) |

The period selector applies to every view. Use a longer period when a project has low traffic so clusters and rates have enough volume.

## Summary

The Summary view gives you the operating picture for the selected period:

- Total sessions and messages
- Deflection rate when turns have intent tags
- Helpful rate on Business and above, based on widget feedback
- Channel breakdown by surface
- Shortcuts into Sessions with filters already applied

Use Summary first, then drill into the view that explains the number.

## Drill into sessions

Most cards link to **Sessions**. For example, a needs-support card opens the matching sessions so you can read the exact questions, citations, and assistant replies behind the metric.

This is the fastest way to decide whether a problem is missing content, stale content, poor source grouping, or a question that should go to support.

## Export and sync

Business and above can export deflection metrics from the Deflection view.

For full conversation sync, use the [Public API](/integrations/public-api) from a trusted backend and paginate `/v1/conversations` and `/v1/messages`.

## Weekly digest

Business and above can enable a weekly email digest from **Settings** (Weekly digest card). The digest summarizes usage, top questions, coverage gaps, and plan usage for workspace admins.
