# Sources cited

Sources cited shows which indexed documents appear in answers during the selected period.

Open **Project > Analytics > Sources**.

## What you see

For each document, Knoku shows how often it was cited and how much of the answer load it carried.

Use this view to find:

- Pages that answer a large share of questions
- Pages that are rarely cited
- Pages that are indexed but not being retrieved
- Source groups that may be too broad or too restricted

Unused documents are not always bad. They may be reference material, niche content, or pages for a workflow users rarely ask about. Investigate before deleting.

## Common fixes

| Signal | Possible fix |
| --- | --- |
| Important page is never cited | Improve title, headings, and surrounding context |
| Private content appears only in team tools | Confirm it belongs to the Internal group |
| Public widget misses a page | Confirm the document is in the Public group |
| Old pages keep answering | Recrawl, resync, or delete stale source content |

## Open the document

Use **Documents** to inspect a cited item, deactivate it, adjust source groups, or rerun the source that owns it.
