# Notion

Notion sync indexes pages shared with your Notion internal integration.

Use it for team runbooks, product notes, support playbooks, and internal knowledge that has not been published as docs.

## Connect Notion

1. Create a Notion [internal integration](https://www.notion.so/help/create-integrations-with-the-notion-api) and copy the integration secret.
2. Open **Project -> Settings -> Integrations -> Notion**.
3. Paste the integration secret and connect.
4. Share the pages or databases you want Knoku to read with that integration in Notion.
5. Open the Notion source on **Project -> Sources** and set sync filters (pages, databases, updated-after rules).
6. Save the filter to start indexing.

Connected pages appear in **Documents** after the first sync.

## Visibility

Notion pages default to Internal. Use the **In widget** toggle on the source row only after reviewing the content and citation URL.

If a Notion page has a public URL and is safe for visitors, enable widget exposure. Otherwise keep it internal for team/support use.

## Sync behavior

Use **Re-index** after changing filters or when you need a fresh pull. Background sync keeps matched pages current.
